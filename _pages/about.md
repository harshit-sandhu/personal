I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, working under the guidance of Prof. Rajesh Chaunsali. My research is driven by a fascination with how we can manipulate the fundamental nature of wave propagation through engineered mechanical systems.

My work sits at the intersection of Solid Mechanics, Topological Physics, and Lattice Dynamics. I am particularly interested in Non-Hermitian systems and Time-Modulated Metamaterials, where the traditional "rules" of reciprocity and energy conservation are intentionally bypassed to achieve unprecedented control over vibrations.

**Research interests:** solid mechanics; vibrations and wave propagation; lattice dynamics and mechanical metamaterials; non-Hermitian systems and phononic time crystals. My doctoral thesis is titled *Dynamics of Elastic Time Crystals*.

My research has been presented at the APS Global Physics Summit (2026) and Metamaterials 2025 in Amsterdam, where I was supported by a conference travel grant and an IISc GARP Travel Fellowship. Alongside research, I mentor students at the LEMS lab on projects such as mechanical neural networks and physical reservoir computing, and I served as the Aerospace Engineering department representative on the IISc Student Council (2024–2026).
