title: Nonreciprocal and non-Hermitian lattices
description: Elastic lattices with active, nonreciprocal stiffness and engineered damping that break the usual symmetry of wave transmission.

In ordinary (passive) elastic lattices, waves travel the same way in both directions. Introducing **nonreciprocal stiffness** — couplings that act differently forwards and backwards, realizable with active elements — together with **engineered damping** makes the system _non-Hermitian_. Such lattices can amplify waves in one direction and attenuate them in the other, and can show effects with no passive counterpart.

I study how nonreciprocity and dissipation combine to shape dispersion, directional amplification and wave localization in discrete elastic lattices.
