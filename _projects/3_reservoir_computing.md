Mechanical systems with rich dynamics can process information directly in their motion. In _physical reservoir computing_, a nonlinear dynamical system — here, a network of active mechanical oscillators — transforms input signals, and only a simple readout is trained. Related ideas lead to _mechanical neural networks_, where the structure itself learns.

This is a newer direction in the LEMS lab, where I mentor student projects that build on our work on active and time-modulated systems:

- Mechanical neural networks and physical reservoir computing in active systems (with Jayalakshmi K, M.Tech Research, IISc).
- Active mechanical oscillators for physical reservoir computing (with Pranav M N, summer intern from IIT Madras, 2026).
- Wave propagation in a 1D chain with a local active site (with Vaishnavi Devgire, summer intern from IIEST Shibpur, 2025).
