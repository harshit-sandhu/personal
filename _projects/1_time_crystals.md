When the stiffness of an elastic medium is modulated periodically in **time** rather than in space, the medium becomes a _phononic time crystal_. The roles of frequency and wavenumber swap: instead of frequency bandgaps, the system opens gaps in wavenumber (momentum), and waves inside those gaps grow or decay in time.

This theme is the core of my doctoral thesis, _Dynamics of Elastic Time Crystals_. I study:

- **Temporal band topology in dissipative systems:** how damping changes the topological character of time-periodic bands.
- **Temporal topological interface states:** localized-in-time states that appear when two time-crystal phases are joined, observed experimentally in a parametric oscillator.
- **Data-driven characterization:** identifying the governing equations of a parametric oscillator from measurements using sparse regression (SINDy).
