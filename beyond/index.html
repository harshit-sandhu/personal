Beyond Research

Interests outside the lab, and service and outreach.

Travel and exploration

Mountain treks

Long days on mountain trails are a counterweight to theory: slow, physical progress, where the reward comes only at the top.

Road trips

Exploring new places by road clears the mind and brings a fresh perspective back to the desk.

Music, books and food

Singing and keyboard

A voice is a resonator too. Pitch, harmonics and timbre are the same wave physics I study, heard rather than measured.

Reading and cooking

Reading widely, and cooking: two experiments with reliably good outcomes.

Body and mind

Yoga

A daily practice that keeps the body balanced and the mind steady through long research cycles.

Swimming

Regular laps, a good way to reset between experiments and writing.
